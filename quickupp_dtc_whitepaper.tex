\begin{abstract}
Direct-to-consumer (DTC) performance advertising on short-form paid social platforms (Meta, Instagram, TikTok) is constrained by rapid ad creative fatigue and prohibitive physical production costs. This paper outlines the operational and mathematical framework of \textbf{Quickupp AI Studio}—leveraging multimodal AI production to achieve exponential creative variations, high hook rates ($\ge 70\%$), and optimal return on ad spend (ROAS) without traditional physical studio overhead.
\end{abstract}
